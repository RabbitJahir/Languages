Basic Operations
--------------------------------

Sum Problems
--------------------------------
6. Total Sum: Find the sum of all elements.

7. Row Sum: Print the sum of every row.

8. Column Sum: Print the sum of every column.

9. Main Diagonal Sum: Sum where i == j.

10. Secondary Diagonal Sum: Sum where i + j == n-1.

11. Boundary Sum: Sum only border elements.

12. Upper Triangle Sum: Sum elements above the main diagonal.

13. Lower Triangle Sum: Sum elements below the main diagonal.
